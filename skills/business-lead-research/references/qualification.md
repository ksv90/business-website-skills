# Qualification and website handoff

## Gates before scoring

Exclude from the active offer: out-of-area/niche, confirmed closed, duplicate, suppressed,
or an existing working site when the campaign is specifically for no-site prospects.
Use review for uncertain identity, blocked website verification, or missing usable
business contact. A contact is usable for planning; it is not consent to advertise.
Never send unsolicited messages just because this skill found a public phone.

Default transparent score (adapt only with an explicit campaign rubric):

- Website opportunity 0–40: not_found/social_only = 40; verified broken/parked = 30
  only when the campaign includes recovery; present/unknown = 0.
- Offer fit 0–25: clear service, area and visitor action fitting the selected scope.
- Evidence completeness 0–20: 5 each for matched identity, contact, required search
  log and current business activity. Unknown evidence gets no points.
- Demo readiness 0–15: 5 each for verified service descriptions, usable licensed
  imagery or an honest typography-led alternative, and a verified contact action.

Record the component rationale. Score measures research priority, not purchase
probability or revenue. Qualification is a gate, not a score threshold. Do not turn
ratings, personal characteristics or presumed wealth into willingness-to-pay signals.

## Handoff contract v1

Create one brief per selected lead with these sections (JSON or equivalent document):

- `schema_version: 1`, `lead_id`, `business`, `source_urls`, `checked_at`.
- `facts`: values with evidence reference, date and status (verified/conflicting).
  Include services, contacts, address, hours, prices only when observed reliably.
- `unknowns`: missing details and whether they block demo or launch.
- `assets`: path/URL, origin, reuse permission, attribution and permitted purpose.
- `website_verification`: status, official/candidate URLs, checks and limitations.
- `proposal`: visitor goal, proposed scope, niche template direction and CTA;
  explicitly separate this from business-provided facts.
- `demo_constraints`: private/unlisted, demo label, no real form submissions,
  prohibited claims and known integration gaps.
- `launch_dependencies`: ownership approval, domain/hosting, content/asset rights,
  working integrations and any market-specific compliance review.

A listing-only link is sufficient to start research, not to assume all facts are
available. The building agent can consume this contract without the discovery skill.
Avoid passing an entire unfiltered customer database to a website project.

## Optional sales preparation

Prepare an unsent, factual introduction only if requested. Say a personalized example
was prepared once it actually exists; otherwise say it can be prepared. For a demo:
“Подготовил пример сайта для вашей компании. Можно прислать ссылку, чтобы вы оценили
структуру и оформление?” Do not claim to represent the company or invent scarcity,
results, client interest or that a contract already exists.

For an authorized outreach task, confirm applicable local channel/consent requirements
from current authoritative sources and apply opt-outs. Keep scripts, call outcomes and
follow-ups factual. Research authorization alone does not authorize communication.
