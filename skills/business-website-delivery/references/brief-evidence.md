# Brief, facts and permissions

## Inputs and independent operation

Accept a directory/map URL, business name plus locality, research handoff v1, existing
site, or owner brief. A bare name without locality may be ambiguous; resolve identity
before writing business-specific content. A research handoff contains lead_id, facts
with sources/dates, unknowns, assets with rights, website verification, proposal,
demo constraints and launch dependencies. It is helpful, not required.

Open sources and verify that they refer to the same organization/branch. Match phone,
address and brand. Prefer current owner-supplied material for company facts; retain
conflicts for review rather than silently choosing the more marketable version.
If a listing is inaccessible, use another permitted source, an owner document or the
user's manual browser completion. Never treat a CAPTCHA as permission to invent facts.

## Evidence ledger

For every material claim record value, source URL/document, observation date and status:
verified, proposed, conflicting or unknown. “Proposed” is a design/copy suggestion,
not a business fact. Critical fields: services, prices and units, hours, location,
coverage, contacts, credentials, guarantees, statistics and testimonials.

Copy can explain a verified service in plain language. It cannot add unverified
“24/7”, “10 years”, “best”, “licensed”, “from 500 ₽”, “free visit” or “100% guarantee”.
Preserve qualifiers, currency, units and effective dates on published prices. If
prices are missing, omit the block or use “Уточните стоимость” without promising
free estimates. Do not fabricate FAQ answers about policies or service conditions.

Reviews must have source, accurate attribution and a permitted reuse basis. Do not
invent people, ratings or results; do not cherry-pick in a misleading way. A provider's
official widget may be an option after checking terms and performance implications.
No review block is better than unsupported social proof.

## Images and brand

Public listing photos are references, not automatically reusable website assets.
Use owner-provided licensed assets, appropriately licensed stock or clearly generic
illustrations. Record rights/attribution for each asset and font. Do not label stock
people as the actual team or generated interiors as the actual premises. If no safe
imagery exists, use a typography-led design. Keep logos provisional until ownership
and usage are confirmed. Do not hotlink another site's assets as a licensing shortcut.

## Missing information

Separate demo blockers from launch blockers. A missing price or logo rarely blocks a
credible demo; missing identity prevents personalization, and missing production form
destination prevents a live form. Complete independent design and coding while a
specific missing fact is requested. Keep internal unknowns out of customer-facing
copy; use omission or honest language instead of leaking editing notes into the design.
